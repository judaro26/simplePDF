PDF files and local processing

For tools marked available, the website reads selected files in your browser and creates the result locally for download. This version has no application server endpoint for uploading or storing your documents, and it does not create an account or cloud document history. Selected file data is held in browser memory while the page is open. Generated downloads are handled by your browser. Your browser, device backups, extensions, or other software may separately retain or access files.

Do not use this site for a document unless you are comfortable processing it on your own device and understand your browser environment. Some operations may fail on encrypted, malformed, unusual, or large files. The “local processing” design is not a security certification or a guarantee about every browser or device.

Information handled by the website and its providers

The app itself does not intentionally set tracking cookies, use analytics, or send document contents to a ClearPDF backend in this version. However, visiting a website necessarily involves network requests. Netlify and any DNS, CDN, security, or infrastructure providers you configure may receive and retain information such as IP address, request time, requested URL, browser/user-agent details, and security logs under their own terms and retention practices. Confirm those practices and list the actual providers here before launch.

The current sponsor area is a visual placeholder: no ad network is integrated. Storage, retention, and security

ClearPDF does not maintain a server-side file repository in this version. Ordinary hosting and security logs may be retained by infrastructure providers. Document files or results saved by you remain under your control and may remain in your downloads folder, browser cache, backups, or device. We cannot promise that all copies are erased from your device or from provider logs.
